OceanEmbed — Elite Research Dashboard Frontend Prompt

Build a premium, highly polished, responsive frontend for a research project called OceanEmbed.

Project

OceanEmbed: Satellite Embedding-Based Deep Learning Framework for Reconstruction of Subsurface Ocean Temperature from Surface Satellite Observations

OceanEmbed uses satellite surface observations and deep-learning-based embeddings to reconstruct subsurface ocean temperature at different depths.

The website should feel like a real scientific/oceanographic research platform, NOT a generic AI dashboard.

The design should communicate:

Oceanography + Scientific Research + Data Visualization + Premium Technology
